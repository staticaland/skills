# Spaghetti pomodoro with garlic and olive oil

A bright, quick sauce in the style of Marcella Hazan's fresh tomato sauce.
It serves 4 and takes about 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 400 g (14 oz) spaghetti
- 1 can (800 g / 28 oz) whole San Marzano tomatoes, or 1 kg ripe summer
  tomatoes
- 4-5 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- A large handful of fresh basil
- Salt, plus 1/2 tsp sugar if the tomatoes taste sharp
- Parmigiano-Reggiano or pecorino, to serve

## Method

1. Crush the tomatoes by hand and discard the tough cores.
2. Warm half the olive oil with the garlic and chili over medium-low heat
   until the garlic turns pale gold, 1-2 minutes. Do not let it brown.
3. Add the tomatoes and 1 tsp salt. Simmer gently for 15-20
   minutes, until the oil floats free and the sauce thickens but still tastes
   fresh. Stir in half the basil near the end.
4. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water and pull it 2 minutes before the
   package time. Save a mug of the pasta water.
5. Move the pasta into the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss over
   medium heat for 1-2 minutes, until the sauce clings and turns glossy.
6. Off the heat, add the rest of the oil, the rest of the basil, and a
   handful of cheese. Toss again and serve.

## Notes

- Finish the pasta in the sauce. The starch in the pasta water binds the
  sauce into a silky coating.
- Add the last of the oil raw, for a fresh, fruity finish.
- Leave out the onion and dried herbs. This sauce should taste of tomato and
  basil.
- In peak tomato season, skip the cooking: marinate chopped ripe tomatoes
  for 30 minutes with oil, garlic, salt, and basil, then toss with hot
  pasta.
