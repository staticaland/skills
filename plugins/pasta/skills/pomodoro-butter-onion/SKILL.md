# Spaghetti pomodoro with butter and onion

Marcella Hazan's tomato sauce with onion and butter: three ingredients in one
pot, with no chopping and no browning. It serves 4 and takes about 1 hour,
most of it unattended.

## Ingredients

- 400 g (14 oz) spaghetti
- 1 can (800 g / 28 oz) whole peeled San Marzano tomatoes, with their juice
- 5 tbsp (70 g) unsalted butter
- 1 medium onion, peeled and halved
- Salt
- Parmigiano-Reggiano, to serve

## Method

1. Put the tomatoes, butter, onion, and 1/2 tsp salt in a saucepan.
2. Simmer uncovered at a slow, steady pace for 45 minutes. Stir from time to
   time and mash any large pieces of tomato against the pot with a wooden
   spoon. The sauce is done when the fat floats free of the tomato.
3. Discard the onion. Taste and adjust the salt.
4. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water and pull it 2 minutes before the
   package time. Save a mug of the pasta water.
5. Move the pasta into the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss over
   medium heat for 1-2 minutes, until the sauce clings.
6. Serve with grated cheese.

## Notes

- The onion sweetens the sauce and turns soft enough to eat as a cook's
  treat. Discard it for a pure tomato flavor.
- The butter makes the sauce rounder and milder than the garlic and olive oil
  version.
- For a creamy finish, stir in a spoonful of mascarpone at the end.
